Clean causal chain (same protocol): R -> V11b improves point MAE 10.2% but makes
delta MAE WORSE by 4.9%, i.e. all of bank's abs_mae gain was amplitude, shape was
actively degraded. Adding the shape term is the first thing that actually teaches
shape. Amplitude and shape are near-orthogonal objectives.

Degeneracy ruled out: new outputs/diag_shape_floor.py shows the per-channel-constant
predictor floor is 0.3212 (moving channels); all arms sit at 0.64-0.70x that floor
and ||dpred||/||dtarget|| = 0.76-0.85, and shape corr rises - so the delta-MAE win
is real tracking, not a flattened curve (a mild ~10% flattening does contribute).

lambda margin: 20 -> 50 costs 6.5% more point MAE for only 0.4% more delta MAE
(0.1428 -> 0.1422). Shape gain saturates on a very shallow plateau => residual shape
error is structural, not a weighting problem. lambda=20 is the operating point;
next sweep is 5/10/15, and the shape term should be masked to moving channels only.

Decision: V11b stays the abs_mae baseline; V11c(lam=20) enters as the "shape-first"
operating point. The acceptance gate must gain a shape criterion - note V11b, the
current SOTA, FAILS such a criterion, which is itself the finding.
